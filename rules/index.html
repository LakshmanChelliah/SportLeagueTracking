Rules

GD RA Volleyball League · 2026–27.

01

Three games, one hour

Play 3 games in the hour. At 10 minutes to the hour the game stops and the team ahead wins. Each game won is 1 point.

02

Rally to 21

Rally scoring to 21. If tied, play until a team leads by 2, or until a team reaches 25 first.

03

Co-ed roster

Register at least 8 players, including at least 2 women. One woman must always be on the court.

04

Minimum on court

Four players or fewer is an automatic loss of the whole match.

05

Serve limit

A player serves at most 5 in a row, then the team rotates and a new server starts.

06

Clear the gym

Games end at 10 to the hour so the next teams can warm up and nets can come down. Out by the end of the slot, or the league can lose the gym.

07

Equipment room

Leave it clean. The last game puts nets and poles away. If the room is a mess when you arrive, tell the coordinator so it can be reported.

08

Season

Wednesday, October 7, 2026 through Wednesday, May 5, 2027. 28 nights. This board lists play through December 2.
